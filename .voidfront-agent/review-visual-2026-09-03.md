## Findings sent for immediate integration

1. **High — battlefield value hierarchy is inverted.** The actual runtime floor is a large bright mint rectangle, and ceramic bodies render almost white. Brass, bevels and team accents are substantially less readable than in the Blender preview. At RTS distance the grouped walkers merge into a bright knot. Reduce runtime light/exposure and floor value; keep strong, intentional team regions. Verify in a fresh runtime capture rather than relying on the Blender render.
2. **High — living and dead units are hard to distinguish.** The end-of-smoke defeat screenshot contains bright, apparently intact corpses below the surviving orange group. HP bars differ but silhouettes/materials remain similar. De-emphasize wrecks after the death clip (lower their luminance or settle/fade them); preserve active combatants as the strongest tactical silhouettes. This finding does not assert the death animation failed: a still cannot prove its deformation or motion.
3. **Medium — outcome text loses contrast.** The amber SIGNAL LOST label sits directly on the bright floor. Use a dark outcome panel/scrim and separate the result from the restart action. Existing top and bottom HUD panels are legible; the result should meet the same standard.
4. **Medium — normal play retains unbounded telemetry.** `frame_times` and `sim_times` append every frame/tick and are not cleared by restart. Restrict collection to smoke/capture or use bounded statistics. This is a source-inspected long-session risk, not an observed leak measurement.

## RTS interaction assessment

The smoke JSON proves programmatically injected F2 selection, RMB move, S stop, and A+click attack-move were accepted; it reports movement, damage, and no drift in the short stop interval. It does not exercise click/drag selection, Shift behavior, camera/zoom, H hold, restart, or human input-to-feedback timing. `RMB` currently always submits Move, so clicking an enemy does not issue a focused attack; do not describe this as complete context orders/targeting. Control groups, queued orders, production/economy, fog and complete matches are absent. Minimap has no camera viewport indicator or click navigation. The bottom panel identifies a unit count but gives no unit attributes or command buttons. These are concrete missing RTS capabilities, not cosmetic objections.

Screenshot shows a tiny single-type skirmish on a rectangular slab with grid, repeated box blockers and no authored terrain. No readable elevation/terrain material system, environmental storytelling, production VFX, audio or differentiated unit roster is evidenced. Blender preview establishes an original articulated industrial walker silhouette with ceramic/brass/team surfaces, but authored surface detail and battle-distance shading need much more work. No SC2-quality claim is supported.

## Performance evidence and limitations

Debug smoke: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2070 SUPER renderer; frame interval p95 16.7003 ms, p99 18.265 ms; bridge advance p95 0.262 ms and p99 0.415 ms; static-memory maximum 46,347,471 bytes. Frame measurements use `_process(delta)`, so they include scheduling/vsync interval and are **not CPU frame duration**. Simulation measurements time `bridge.advance()` and exclude snapshot extraction/presentation. Godot MEMORY_STATIC_MAX is **not process peak resident memory**. The observed frame interval p99 exceeds the 16.67 ms target, but this debug, tiny-skirmish capture neither establishes representative performance nor isolates the bottleneck. Hardware differs from SPEC reference; GPU is observed in the renderer, RAM remains unverified. Need optimized runtime, documented sample duration/warmup, CPU/GPU frame profiling, resident-memory sampling and 200/500-unit scenarios before acceptance.
